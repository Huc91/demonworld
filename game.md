#general

The videogame is 2d top perspetctive of an rpg like pokemon.
The world is open world. You go around cities and wilderness (copy maps from pokemon , zelda and other rpg) set in a modern world. The game revolves around cards, in specific a card game tcg called "Devil Summoner". The whole world is crazy for this game. You are a 30y old who found in an old shoes box his collection. 

# the videogame
- open world structure
- when you find an enemy a card fight starts.
- you collect cards.
- when you defeat an enemy you receive money or sometimes a card. 
- with money you buy booster packs.
- scope: find and defeat all 8 bosses. then the final boss.
- you can find rare cards hidden in the most not seen spots in the map.
- zelda / metroidvania some area accessibile with specifict power obtained from bosses or places. 
- quests

# the cardgame
- usually best of 1 fights.
- fast fights
- deck of 30 cards.
- every player roll a die, higest result goes first. Tie reroll until one wins.
- Who goes second start with an extra mana free mana.
- draw 5 start of game
- you have 10 life points.
- every card has a cost
- to play a card you must pay the cost
- every card can be played to generate 1 mana, 2mana or 3 mana, depends on the card or be played for his card effect. Mana is used to pay the cost to play cards.
- you summon demons that have HP and an ATK stats, is like heartstone.
- you draw 5 cards at the end of turn, not played cards get discarded. you do not automatically draw a card at start of turn, only at end turn redraw 5.
